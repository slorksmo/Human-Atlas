# Anatomy coverage and source research

Audited on 2026-11-18 against the Study branch manifests. The male reference has 2,238 selectable meshes and 3,437 named concepts. The female reference has 1,252 meshes and 1,471 concepts. A concept can describe a group or a subdivision of a mesh. The coverage audit checks selected names and spelling variants in both collections and preserves matching IDs and source-layer names.

## First tasks

1. **Expand the salivary-gland lesson from existing geometry.** Both references have two submandibular and two sublingual meshes. The female reference also has both parotid glands. Revise the current lesson summary so the parotid limitation applies to the male reference, and use the female parotid meshes in a body-specific extension. Resolve equivalent names to the correct concept per body and test every study link in both languages. No new geometry is needed.
2. **Pilot one masseter before importing a jaw-muscle pack.** Neither atlas has masseter, temporalis, or medial pterygoid meshes under the checked names. The SPL head-and-neck index lists separate left and right masseter and temporalis VTK files, plus both parotids. Start with a single masseter and matching mandible/zygomatic landmarks, check the notice included in the downloaded package under Slicer Part B, and test surface fit and mobile payload in an opt-in layer. The SPL index search identified no medial pterygoid mesh. [SPL package](https://www.openanatomy.org/atlas-pages/atlas-spl-head-and-neck.html) · [Model index](https://www.openanatomy.org/atlases/nac/head-neck-2016-09/atlasStructure.json) · [Slicer license](https://github.com/Slicer/Slicer/blob/main/License.txt)
3. **Add a small, separate inner-ear lesson.** The checked cochlea, bony labyrinth and semicircular canal names found no meshes. Wimmer et al. publish PLY surface data from 23 specimens and landmarks under CC BY 4.0. Pick one documented specimen and extract just its surface and landmark metadata. Start with a standalone organ lesson so whole-body placement is a separate test. The bony labyrinth describes the inner-ear space and does not supply middle-ear ossicles. [Dataset and files](https://zenodo.org/records/3355272) · [Methods paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6864122/)

## Other gaps and decisions

- **Female skeleton.** The present talus, calcaneus, scapula and clavicle results belong to the male-derived layer on the female body. BoneHub provides CT-based female labels under CC BY 4.0. Its cranium and maxilla are grouped, its elbow is incomplete, and most sesamoids are absent. Continue the existing left-ankle work by reviewing the articular landmarks before replacing borrowed meshes. Compare the small pilot against the atlas body surface and record the transform, rather than importing the whole skeleton. Credit Azevedo79z for the inventory and first overlay, and credit Alavi and Asseln and Andreassen et al. for the data as required by the dataset. [Upstream inventory PR](https://github.com/slorksmo/Human-Atlas/pull/1) · [Initial overlay measurements](https://github.com/Azevedo79z/Human-Atlas/blob/feat/female-left-ankle-native-overlay/docs/FEMALE-LEFT-ANKLE-LOCAL-SCREENING-RUN8.md) · [Dataset and limitations](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BoneHub/visible-human-3d-models)
- **Female shoulder and upper-limb muscles.** No biceps brachii or deltoid mesh matched in the female atlas. Gluteus maximus is present in the existing female donor layer. The University of Utah shoulder deposit offers CC BY 4.0 scapula/humerus STL meshes, demographics and landmarks. The population includes pathological samples, so select a documented healthy, paired female shoulder for a registration study. A separate CC BY 4.0 release offers muscle paths and attachment/intermediate surfaces. Those are useful landmarks, with complete muscle meshes still to be verified. [Utah shoulder bones](https://zenodo.org/records/19077748) · [Muscle attachment geometry](https://zenodo.org/records/3685622)
- **Jaw and dental limits.** Both bodies have lateral pterygoid reference meshes from University of Dundee, with four components per body. The audit found no names for a TMJ articular disc or third permanent molar. MIDA has the relevant jaw-muscle labels and STL geometry, but clause 2.3.2 prohibits redistribution of original and modified model data. Dental Scope documents four modeled third molars derived from neighbouring second molars. It is useful as a teaching-design reference and does not establish subject-derived third-molar geometry. Keep separately evidenced third molars or an explicitly illustrative tooth lesson as follow-up work. [MIDA model](https://itis.swiss/virtual-population/regional-human-models/mida-model) · [MIDA terms](https://itis.swiss/assets/Downloads/VirtualPopulation/License_Agreements/LicenseAgreementMIDA_2024.pdf) · [Dental Scope credits](https://github.com/Yoosseph/dental-scope/blob/main/CREDITS.md)
- **Middle ear.** OpenEar's open CC BY 4.0 release contains eight specimens. Its methods specify separate PLY meshes for malleus, incus, and stapes, plus the facial nerve and tympanic membrane. Start with the three ossicles from a single specimen and keep them in the same coordinate frame. The full archives contain images as well as meshes. Keep imaging volumes out of the web app. The SPL inner-ear atlas is a second option if its exact package terms and contents suit the lesson. [OpenEar data](https://zenodo.org/records/1473724) · [OpenEar methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6326113/) · [SPL Inner Ear Atlas](https://www.openanatomy.org/atlas-pages/atlas-spl-inner-ear.html)
- **Lymphatics and HRA releases.** No thoracic-duct match appeared in the current manifests. The HRA catalog exposes individual female and male lymph-node v1.4 GLBs and crosswalk files. That is a candidate for a focused lymph-node lesson and naming improvements. A full-body lymphatic-network layer still needs geometry evidence for named vessels and anatomical placement. Compare object versions and crosswalk IDs before assuming a newer HRA release changes a packaged body. [HRA object library](https://humanatlas.io/3d-reference-library) · [HRA Knowledge Graph and license](https://github.com/hubmapconsortium/hra-kg)

## Repeat the research

Run from the repository root with the project's supported Node.js version.

```sh
node scripts/audit-anatomy.mjs --output anatomy-coverage.json
node scripts/audit-anatomy.mjs --discover --baseline docs/anatomy-research-baseline.json --output anatomy-discovery.json
```

The first command reads the checked-in manifests offline. The second also checks the five source endpoints and searches recent DataCite dataset registrations using the queries in [anatomy-research.json](anatomy-research.json). A hit is a lead. The DOI, source file, and license must be reviewed before using it. Metadata-only changes and false positives are expected. A failed request is reported explicitly and returns a nonzero exit code, with any partial report still available.

After the workflow is on the default branch, run **Actions → Anatomy research → Run workflow** whenever needed. GitHub also schedules it on the third of each month. Download the **anatomy-research** artifact. The workflow reports changes relative to the checked-in baseline. Update [anatomy-research-baseline.json](anatomy-research-baseline.json) in a reviewed commit after examining a change. The workflow reads public metadata and writes report artifacts; it never fetches model packages or changes the atlas.

For broader discovery, query DataCite, Zenodo, university repositories and GitHub forks with the region, anatomical synonyms, sex or side when relevant, and format terms such as STL, PLY, VTK or GLB. Useful queries include `"masseter" "temporalis" surface mesh`, `"female upper extremity" muscle segmentation`, and `"thoracic duct" 3D surface dataset`. Check the publisher's model index and per-file rights; an open-access paper or a public viewer can have different terms from its geometry.

Record each candidate with the following evidence before proposing integration.

- **Coverage** includes the relevant atlas reference, existing concept or mesh IDs, and the exact candidate structures and sides.
- **Source and credits** include creator, version, file URL, hash, explicit geometry license, required notices and the fork contributor or prior work that led to it.
- **Fit** includes units, axes, subject and pose, matched landmarks, overlaps, and anatomical review. Keep parts from a different subject identified in the viewer.
- **Preview** includes a small representative import, attribution in the shipped viewer, mobile payload and interaction checks, and a reviewer decision. Expand coverage based on that result.
